{% if zh %}常见问题{% else %}FAQ{% endif %}

{% if zh %}
支持哪些格式？

目前只支持 TXT。月读会自动识别常见编码和章节，不改写原文件。

收费吗？

免费，没有订阅，所有功能都能用。设置里有完全自愿的打赏，打赏不会解锁任何东西。

AI 要付费吗？

AI 是可选功能，使用你自己选择的服务和账户，费用和额度由该服务决定。不用 AI 也能正常阅读。

换手机后书和进度还在吗？

把小说放在“我的书架”并开启 iCloud Drive，文件会同步；在设置中开启阅读数据 iCloud 同步后，进度、书签和批注也会同步。

支持 iPad 吗？

支持 iPhone 和 iPad，需要 iOS 18 或更新版本。

{% else %}
Which formats are supported?

TXT only for now. Moonread detects common encodings and chapters and never rewrites your files.

Does it cost anything?

It is free with no subscription, and every feature is available. Settings include completely optional tips that unlock nothing.

Does AI cost money?

AI is optional and uses the service and account you choose, so pricing and quota come from that service. Reading works fine without it.

Will my books and progress move to a new phone?

Keep books in My Bookshelf with iCloud Drive on and the files sync. Turn on reading-data iCloud sync in Settings to sync positions, bookmarks, and notes too.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes, on iPhone and iPad running iOS 18 or later.
